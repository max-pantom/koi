# Code of Conduct

Koi should be a welcoming place to learn, share, and build together.

## Our standards

Please:

- Be kind, patient, and respectful
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience
- Give useful feedback about the work, not the person
- Accept thoughtful feedback and take responsibility for mistakes

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing someone’s private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, [email the project maintainer](mailto:metagravity0@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept private as far as reasonably possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing Koi in public spaces.
